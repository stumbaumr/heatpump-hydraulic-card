- New option `unit_pump` (also in the editor, default from the preset): draws the heat pump's own water pump on the flow pipe next to the heat pump block, centred under the flow temperature and over the flow rate. It turns while the flow rate is above 0, else with the heating or hot-water loop. A single direct circuit without a `hc_pump` sensor then shows no pump of its own. The HeishaMon preset turns it on, because the Aquarea water pump sits in the outdoor unit (monobloc) or the hydro module (split) and also runs for hot water.
- Heating curve shift: the flow temperature under the circuit comes only from the circuit's own flow sensor (`hc_flow`). Without one the heat pump's flow stands only at the VL label and is not shown twice.
