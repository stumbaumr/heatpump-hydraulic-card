- I added a Bosch/Buderus Home Comfort preset for the PointT cloud integration, using stable registry identities for measurements, statistics and details. The default controls are limited to the selected heating and DHW circuit modes and one-time DHW charge; further controls are opt-in. The card and editor share registry loading and check only the integration's registered states during updates. I included a mapping guide, synthetic regression checks and an upstream manifest. The automatic preset still needs its own installation test.
